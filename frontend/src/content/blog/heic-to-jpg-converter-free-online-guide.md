<h3 style="text-align: left; color: #ffffff;">Why Convert HEIC to JPG Instead of Just Changing the Extension?</h3>
<!-- /block:heading -->

<!-- block:paragraph {"fontSize":"normal","align":"left","textColor":"#ffffff"} -->
<p style="text-align: left; color: #ffffff;">A common mistake people make is simply renaming a .heic file to .jpg. This does not work. Renaming a file doesn't change how the image data is encoded inside it — it just changes the label. The file will still be unreadable by anything that doesn't support the HEIC codec.</p>
<!-- /block:paragraph -->

<!-- block:paragraph {"fontSize":"normal","align":"left","textColor":"#ffffff"} -->
<p style="text-align: left; color: #ffffff;">To properly convert HEIC to JPG, the image data itself needs to be decoded from the HEIC format and re-encoded as a genuine JPEG. That's exactly what a real HEIC to JPG converter does.</p>
