Naye saal mein travel planning ho ya official visa processing, ek sahi aur valid passport photo hona sabse critical requirement hai. Har saal hazaron passport aur visa applications sirf isliye reject ho jati hain kyonki photo ka size, background, ya head-alignment government rules se match nahi karta.

2026 mein physical passport applications aur digital e-Visa forms dono ke liye regulations kafi strict ho chuke hain. Is guide mein, hum alag-alag countries (India, US, UK, Schengen) ke official passport photo size requirements aur guidelines ko samjhenge, taaki aapki application pehli baar mein hi pass ho jaye.

## 2026 Ke Standard Passport Photo Dimensions Kya Hain?

Alag-alag deshon ke liye passport photo ke dimensions alag hote hain. Chaliye unhe systematically dekhte hain:

### 1. India Passport Photo Requirements
Indian passport aur OCI (Overseas Citizenship of India) applications ke liye physical photo dimensions niche diye gaye hain:
- **Dimensions:** 2 inch x 2 inch (51 mm x 51 mm ya 5.1 cm x 5.1 cm).
- **Format:** Square ratio (1:1).
- **Resolution (Digital uploads ke liye):** Minimum 350px x 350px aur maximum 1000px x 1000px.
- **Head Size:** Chin (thoddi) se lekar head ke top tak ka area photo ke total area ka 70% se 80% hona chahiye.

### 2. United States (US) Passport & Visa Requirements
US passport aur visa applications ke rules kafi strict hote hain:
- **Dimensions:** 2 inch x 2 inch (51 mm x 51 mm).
- **Digital Photo Format:** Square format, minimum 600px x 600px aur maximum 1200px x 1200px resolution.
- **Background:** Strictly plain off-white ya white background.

### 3. United Kingdom (UK) Passport Requirements
UK passport applications mein rectangular photos standard hoti hain:
- **Dimensions:** 35 mm width x 45 mm height.
- **Aspect Ratio:** Rectangular (7:9 approx).
- **Note:** Photo mein shoulder ka top part aur head clear hona chahiye, aur shoulders slanted nahi hone chahiye.

### 4. Schengen Visa (Europe) Photo Requirements
Schengen states (Germany, France, Italy, Spain, etc.) ke visa applications ke liye standard European guidelines apply hoti hain:
- **Dimensions:** 35 mm width x 45 mm height.
- **Face Coverage:** Face height 32 mm se 36 mm ke beech honi chahiye (around 70-80% of photo).
- **Background:** Neutral light grey ya light blue (white background kuch applications mein reject ho sakta hai agar clothes white hon).

## Passport Photo Ke Liye Rules Aur Guidelines (Do's and Don'ts)

Bina kisi rejection ke passport photo upload ya submit karne ke liye in rules ka dhyan rakhein:

- **Expression:** Aapka expression neutral hona chahiye. Smile na karein, aur eyes bilkul khuli aur camera ki taraf seedhe focus honi chahiye. Mouth closed hona chahiye.
- **Background:** Background hamesha plain white ya light neutral grey hona chahiye. Patterned ya shadows wale backgrounds strictly not allowed hain.
- **Glasses & Headwear:** 2026 ke rules ke mutabik, medical reasons ke bina glasses allowed nahi hain. Caps, hats, ya hairbands strictly forbidden hain. Religious headwear (jaise Hijab ya Turban) allowed hain agar face forehead se chin tak clear dikh raha ho.
- **Lighting:** Face par uniform lighting honi chahiye taaki shadows na banein (especially eyes ke niche ya nose ke side mein). Red-eye filter wale photos use na karein.
